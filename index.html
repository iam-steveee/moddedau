Manual installation
Download regionInfo.jsonManual Installation

Navigate to AppData/LocalLow/Innersloth/Among Us and replace the existing region file.

Method 1: Quick Access

  1. 1
    Copy this path:
    %userprofile%\AppData\LocalLow\Innersloth\Among Us
  2. 2
    Press WIN + R
  3. 3
    Press CTRL + V
  4. 4
    Press ENTER

Method 2: Manual Navigation

  1. 1
    Open File Explorer.
  2. 2
    Enter %APPDATA% in the address bar.
  3. 3
    Press ENTER.
  4. 4
    Open LocalLow.
  5. 5
    Open Innersloth.
  6. 6
    Open Among Us.
Copy regionInfo.json into the folder and replace the existing file when prompted.
Restart Among Us after making the change.
